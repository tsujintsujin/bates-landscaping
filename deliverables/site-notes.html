Notes on bateslandscaping.co

While rebuilding your homepage as a demo, I went through the current site page by page. These are the things I noticed, each with what the remodel does about it. Everything here was checked on the live site.

What already works well

  • Real case studies with county and price range. Few landscapers publish this. It's the strongest proof on the site.
  • Real people. Team bios with consistent headshots in Bates red, and the NALP 2024 Awards of Excellence.
  • It loads quickly. The main content showed in about 1.7 seconds on desktop and 0.7 seconds on a phone in my test.
  • Clear service area. Chester, Delaware and Montgomery Counties, stated consistently.

What I found

On the current siteIn the remodel
1 The homepage service cards have mixed-up descriptions. The Maintenance card talks about water problems, the Drainage card describes snow removal, Snow + Ice talks about water again, and Bamboo repeats the generic company line. Each card uses the opening line from that service's own page.
2 Booking asks for photos but never says where to send them. Book Now, step 3: "Email 3-5 photos of your property to the provided email address." No address appears on the page. Photos upload as part of the booking form, and the booking arrives by email (in progress).
3 "Privacy Policy" opens the SMS terms. The footer link goes to /sms-terms-condition/. Separate privacy policy link, plus a consent-preferences panel.
4 Two different phone numbers. The site shows (484) 887-8678; the trucks and snow photos show (484) 443-8772. One number everywhere, one tap to call on phones. Which is the right one?
5 The email address is hard to find and uses a different domain. info@bateslandscaping.net (.net, while the site is .co) only appears on two inner pages. Email shown in every footer.
6 "One of the highest rated companies", but no reviews anywhere. There are no testimonials or review links on the site to back up the claim. A spot is ready for real reviews (none were invented for the demo).
7 31 FAQs are stacked on the homepage (with 24 more on inner pages). One answer reads "Too many to list here! – check out Our Services page"; the scheduling answer only covers hardscaping projects. Six short FAQs on the homepage, with a chat assistant that answers from all 55 and a full FAQ page in progress.
8 Pages that overlap. Book Now and Request a Quote show the same booking calendar; there are two gallery pages (/project-gallery/ and /project-gallery-tabs/); the Home link is ?page_id=19. One booking page, one gallery, clean addresses. Existing page addresses are kept so search rankings carry over.
9 Repeated and hidden blocks on the homepage. "WE ARE: Bates Landscaping" appears twice, and a "Book Project Consultation Now" block sits in the page code but never shows on desktop. Each section appears once.
10 Case studies share titles. Four different jobs are all called "Bamboo Removal" and two are "Custom Pondless Water Feature", so they look identical in menus and search results. Case studies shown with county, type and price tags so each one is distinct.
11 Gallery photos titled with file names. In a sample of 20, 12 had only a file name as their title (for example "4-2", "123", "untitled-design-76"), which search engines and screen readers read out as-is. Every photo has a short description, such as "Patios, West Chester".
12 Bamboo is missing from the Residential menu, even though three of the four bamboo case studies are homeowners. Bamboo Remediation listed under both Commercial and Residential.
