### Commercial Landscape Maintenance Company

As a business owner, your outdoor spaces should be well-preserved and presentable. Your landscape is the first thing customers, guests, and employees will see when approaching your property. With routine maintenance, you can ensure your outdoor spaces reflect the professionalism of your company.

Bates Landscaping is a reliable provider of professional commercial landscape maintenance services. Whether you own a retail store, an office complex, a restaurant, or another commercial space, our landscapers can handle maintenance at any scale. We personalize your maintenance to meet the unique needs of your landscape, handling everything from lawn care to tree cutting.

## Commercial Lawn Care Services

## Commercial properties often feature expansive lawns that can be difficult to keep under control. Bates Landscaping offers routine lawn care services, helping you keep your landscape vibrant and well-trimmed. Our commercial lawn care services include mowing, aeration, and fertilization. We will work with your company to develop a lawn maintenance plan that operates around your schedule and budget. With a lush lawn, your business will stand out from the crowd. Contact us today to learn more or to schedule a consultation.

## Commercial Tree & Plant Maintenance

## In addition to lawn care, we also specialize in tree and plant maintenance. Through this service, we provide pruning, fertilization, pest control, weed removal, and disease control. Our goal is to foster the sustained health and growth of your flora, ensuring a flourishing landscape all year round. Whether you are looking for routine maintenance or require assistance with a particular issue, you can depend on our team for the professional care you need. Bates Landscaping also provides seasonal commercial landscape maintenance in the fall and spring, making sure your property always looks its best. Our seasonal maintenance services include leaf cleanup, mulching, perennial cutback, and bed edging. Get in touch with us today to begin scheduling your maintenance.

## Commercial Landscape Maintenance for Senior Living Facilities

## Senior living facilities require landscape maintenance to ensure the safety and comfort of their elderly residents. We ensure your property is easy to navigate, promoting the overall well-being of the seniors and encouraging time spent outdoors. A beautiful landscape can also attract new residents.

## Commercial Landscape Maintenance for Property Managers

## Landscape maintenance is an important part of property management. Bates Landscaping helps to keep your property looking its best with routine mowing, pruning, plant care, and seasonal maintenance. This offers the attractive curb appeal you need to satisfy guests and tenants.

## Commercial Landscape Maintenance for Office Buildings

## An attractive landscape offers a relaxing retreat for office spaces. After long hours of sitting indoors, employees and guests can find comfort in well-maintained outdoor spaces. An overgrown landscape can take away from the attractive features of your office space. You can trust Bates Landscaping for an exterior that reflects the professionalism of your company.

## Commercial Landscape Maintenance for Homeowners Association

## As a homeowners association, you are tasked with managing communal spaces for a number of residents. Bates Landscaping provides premier landscape management services to help elevate the appearance of these common areas. Our services help to create a strong sense of community and pride amongst your residents.

## Commercial Landscape Maintenance for Churches & Cemeteries

## Churches and cemeteries are sacred spaces that deserve proper care and attention. Our commercial landscape maintenance services help to provide the serenity required by such properties. Our commercial landscapers maintain your landscape throughout the year, offering a contemplative and tranquil environment for visitors.
